## Experience

1. Brand reference: up to three public website pages, or pasted brand material.
2. Confirm identity, audience, objective, offer, one concrete detail and CTA. A customer question/answer is optional.
3. Separate owner-confirmed observation, hypothesis to test and recommendation. Explain the rule, show real source coverage/dates, allow correction, and offer three approaches.
4. Edit the piece, review every page and asset, approve the current version, and export PNG(s), caption and references as a ZIP. Continue deeper diagnosis in Minha trilha.

## Rules and quality gates

- A supplied customer question recommends education; otherwise booking/launch goals recommend an invitation, brand-strengthening recommends a photo-led post, and acquisition recommends education.
- Education is an actual three-page carousel; trust and invitation are single-page compositions with distinct layouts. All pages and the shared caption are editable.
- Missing customer answers are not invented. Education using a question cannot be approved without a supplied answer. A trust post requires a supplied photo.
- Human edits are preserved on return. Changing approach/context requires explicit confirmation before replacing an existing draft.
- Brief, customer Q&A, all slide text, color, logo and photo participate in versioned approval. Changes revoke approval/export credit.
- Website coverage is not social analytics. No posting frequency, reach, engagement or conversion claims are derived from partial public pages.
- This is local, rule-based composition from confirmed inputs, not semantic AI research or a proven content-performance prediction. External AI processing is not enabled.

## Verification

86 Node tests pass; TypeScript, focused ESLint and local Vercel-preset build pass. Browser smoke test covers a fresh fictional account, manual fallback, recommendation, three-page editing, approval and ZIP download initiation. No production data or networks are published to.

Independent review found and resolved three edge cases: normalized free-text goal matching, orphaned Q&A answers excluded from captions, and a photo layout that reserves readable text space before sizing the image. The replacement-confirmation warning was observed, but automated dismissal hit a browser-control limitation; the operator was asked to cancel the fictional test draft's dialog.
